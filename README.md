## Percy Jackson & the Olympians
 **Percy Jackson** is a fantasy series about a teenage demigod who trains at Camp Half-Blood and goes on quests for the Greek gods.
 
### Main Characters
 - Percy Jackson
 - Annabeth Chase
 - Grover Underwood
 
> "Look, I didn't want to be a half-blood."
 
## The Hunger Games
 **The Hunger Games** is a dystopian series about teens forced to fight in a televised survival contest against a tyrannical government.
 
### Main Characters
 - Katniss Everdeen
 - Peeta Mellark
 - Gale Hawthorne
 
## Stranger Things
 **Stranger Things** is a sci-fi series about kids in a small town who battle supernatural forces from a mysterious parallel dimension.
 
### Main Characters
 - Eleven
 - Mike Wheeler
 - Dustin Henderson
